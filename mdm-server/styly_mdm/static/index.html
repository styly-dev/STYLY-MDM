Power Control▾
⚠ Reboot or power off the selected devices — whatever is running is interrupted. Power off works even while charging, and a powered-off headset stays off and cannot be turned back on remotely — it must be woken physically. Success shows as the device dropping offline (a reboot reconnects on its own after ~30–60s).
Retire Devices▾
⚠ Permanently removes the MDM client (and its guard app) from the selected devices, for final handover. Retired devices go offline and can no longer be managed remotely — recovery requires physical USB access per unit. Pushed content remains, but a startup app set here stops auto-launching once the client is gone.
