# Group Settings specification

## Scope and sources

The comparison covers the entire delta from `suu/issue-94-resumable-downloads` at `b005de1fcaa58de298f9bd666e4e8f38571454c8` through `suu/group-settings` at `22d0bf1db3cf5f14efecfb65364717658668b9a3` plus the applied, uncommitted membership patch. That working delta spans 47 files. It includes the server and console, the Android application and provisioning UI, startup and self-update interactions, tests, documentation and diagrams.

The operating goal is to reuse equipment setup across a managed PICO fleet and observe the results while headsets may be offline or changing networks. The November 9 Group Settings and Configuration Packages decisions in `tasks/todo.md` supersede the October Wi-Fi prototype and the per-group Deploy design. The developer and Wi-Fi guides record the current contracts. The original recipe and complete conversation were unavailable. ## Scenarios chosen from use

1. Publish one setup for two groups, change a member while one is offline, restart the station and reconnect. Observe scope confirmation, latest policy and result display.
2. Apply a package, change it and return to the original in a boot. Restart the process and boot the headset. Count writes, compare recorded evidence and exercise explicit Retry.
3. Switch Wi-Fi with the station unreachable. Drive local success or rollback, restart the process at a stage boundary, and verify that content startup has independent bounded progress.
